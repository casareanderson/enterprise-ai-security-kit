# Enterprise AI Security Kit

A LinkedIn content kit for IT and security practitioners writing about **enterprise AI patterns, trends and risk**.

Twelve short posts, one a week for a quarter. Each teaches one mechanism and closes on something the reader can do on Monday.

**This repository is the free sample: three of the twelve posts, plus the five post structures and every source cited.**

---

## The thesis

The tide has turned. Between 2023 and 2025 the enterprise question was *"can this work?"* In 2026 it is *"who is accountable when it does?"*

That single shift moves AI out of the innovation lab and into the same machinery every other production system lives in: identity, least privilege, change control, logging, and audit. Most enterprise AI risk is **old risk wearing a new interface**.

## How to use it

1. Pick a post. Read the `sources` block in its front matter.
2. **Verify anything in a `[STAT SLOT]` before you publish.** This kit deliberately ships *no unsourced numbers* — where a figure would strengthen a post, it names the report to pull the current figure from instead of inventing one. That discipline is the whole point of the tone.
3. Paste the text between `POST START` and `POST END`. Nothing else goes in the box.
4. Post the link, if any, as the **first comment** — not in the body.
5. Answer every comment for the first 60 minutes.

## The one rule

> If you can't name where a claim comes from, cut the claim. The post is still good without it.

Credibility in security writing is a balance you spend, not a tone you adopt. One invented statistic costs more than ten posts earn.
