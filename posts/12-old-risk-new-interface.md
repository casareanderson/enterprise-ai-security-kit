POST START

Twelve posts on enterprise AI security. One conclusion.

Almost none of it was new.

Prompt injection is the confused deputy. RAG leakage is broken access control. Shadow AI is shadow IT. Model files are dependencies. Agents are non-human identities. Runaway loops are denial of service. Missing prompt logs are missing audit logs.

The interface changed. The failure modes did not.

That should be reassuring, and for most security teams it is not — because the controls were already weak before anything was generating text. AI did not create the gap. It industrialised the consequences of it.

So the roadmap is unglamorous and available today: identity, least privilege, logging, change control, and an owner for every system.

The teams that will be trusted with AI in 2027 are the ones doing that work now, quietly, with no announcement.

The tide has turned. The fundamentals didn't.
