POST START

For three years the enterprise question about AI was: can this work?

That question is closed. The 2026 question is: who is accountable when it does?

That is not a philosophical shift. It is an operational one.

The moment a model influences a decision a customer or a regulator can challenge, it stops being a project and becomes production infrastructure. And production infrastructure has rules that predate AI by thirty years:

— A named owner
— An identity, not a shared key
— Least privilege on everything it can touch
— Change control, with a way back
— Logs an incident responder can actually read

None of that is new. That is the point.

Most enterprise AI risk is old risk wearing a new interface. The teams pulling ahead right now are not the ones with the best model. They are the ones who already knew how to run things.

Where is your AI in that list?
